── Billing Model ──

  Free tier: 1000 translation uses (count-based, not token-based)
  Pro tier:  Unlimited translations
  Admin:     Unlimited translations

  Referral reward: +3000 free translation uses per successful invite

── Notes ──

  1. Admin accounts are identified by email in the ADMIN_EMAILS list
     in both server (admin.ts) and client (auth.js)
  2. Activation codes are stored in Firestore collection "activation_codes"
     and validated server-side via POST /api/activate
  3. Referral system uses invite codes (auto-generated per user)
     tracked in Firestore collection "referrals"
